02 · The Framework

Cinematic Learning Production Framework (CLPF)

v1.0 · Published Foundational Architecture Document, May 2026

A practitioner-theorist framework. It is not empirically validated, does not claim that production quality causes learning, and was developed in enterprise contexts rather than claimed as universal.

Four systems

  1. Strategic & Narrative
  2. World & Production
  3. Cognitive & Behavioral
  4. Enterprise & Scaling

Eight governing axioms (v1.0)

No.AxiomStatus, October 2026
01The Architecture Is the Intervention.
The experience design is the learning intervention, not a delivery vehicle.		Holds

Narrative absorption → cognitive reorganization → emotional investment → intention → transfer.		Holds; enterprise transfer unproven
03Emotional Salience Is Structural.Holds
04The Learner Is Always the Protagonist.Holds

Craft should dissolve the boundary between learner and experience.		Under revision

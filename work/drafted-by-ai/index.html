Executive summary

A readiness strategy tied to the work.

I framed the business goal around resolving AI-invoicing questions and helping customers keep using the feature with confidence. That meant designing for Expert judgment and customer communication together.

I used the customer problem to determine what belonged in practice, what needed support during a call, and what would need reinforcement after launch. The working sample demonstrates selected parts of that strategy.

  1. Business need

    Resolve the issue. Preserve confidence.

    Support customers through an AI-assisted mistake without defaulting to abandoning the feature.

  2. Learning response

    Practice judgment in context.

    Connect product accuracy, customer communication, and support at the moment of need.

  3. Success to evaluate

    Look beyond completion.

    Examine demonstrated skill, workplace behavior, and the quality of customer resolution.

Strategic design decisions

The strategic choices behind the design.

Protect operational capacity.

Experts learn between customer contacts, with limited time away from their queues.

I chose short, modular learning and support available during the work, so every format had a distinct purpose.

Build capability beyond launch.

A feature demonstration alone cannot establish consistent behavior on a customer call.

I connected demonstration, realistic practice, performance support, and reinforcement. I treated product correctness and customer trust as separate dimensions of readiness.

Design for the next feature.

A custom course for every release would repeat design and production effort.

I separated reusable learning structures from feature-specific content. The intent was to make future updates easier without compromising the customer context.

Reflection

The next investment should follow the evidence.

The prototype makes the design approach tangible. It does not yet establish workplace transfer or business impact. My next priority would be to validate the product assumptions and observe how representative Experts use the experience.

Those findings would guide what to refine in practice, what belongs in performance support, and whether the reusable structure is ready for another feature. AI accelerated drafting and production; I retained ownership of the learning decisions and editorial review.

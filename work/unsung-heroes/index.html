04 / Brief & learning architecture

Two audiences.
One sustainable approach.

Students needed production fluency. Their teacher needed a facilitation model that could survive the coach’s departure.

Students

  1. Investigate a historical figure
  2. Write for a listening audience
  3. Record and edit with purpose
  4. Credit sources and share responsibly

Teacher

  1. Observe the essential moves
  2. Join facilitation and student support
  3. Debrief as a peer
  4. Lead the next cycle with support available

08 / Reflection

The handoff is part
of the learning design.

The central design question was not just whether students could make an episode. It was whether classroom capability could continue without the specialist leading every session.

What I would evaluate today

  • Whether the teacher can facilitate the next cycle independently
  • How teams move through production milestones
  • Whether audio choices support researched meaning
  • Which requests for coaching recur
Reconstruction 2026

What still needs evidence

  • Corroboration of the teacher-led repeat and later adaptations
  • Original archive inspection and publication permissions
  • The effect on students with different learning needs
  • Approved media and final transcript timing for this demonstration
